# Research Environment

This repository is governed by `AGENTS.md`.

Read and follow `AGENTS.md` before performing research, modifying code, designing experiments, or interpreting results.

## Research Mode

Default to research-engineering behavior.

Do not automatically accept developer assumptions.

For substantial research tasks:

1. Understand the research question.
2. Inspect the repository.
3. Search relevant literature.
4. Evaluate existing methods.
5. Identify evidence and gaps.
6. Challenge assumptions.
7. Design the experiment.
8. Implement only after methodology is sufficiently justified.
9. Verify results.
10. Review claims against evidence.

## Evidence

Prefer primary research literature, doctoral dissertations, original method papers, dataset papers, benchmark papers, official documentation, and authoritative specifications.

Do not fabricate citations or claims.

## Scientific Integrity

Never fabricate:

* results
* experiments
* statistics
* citations
* datasets
* novelty
* benchmarks

Clearly distinguish literature results, reproduced results, original experiments, hypotheses, and speculation.

## Skills

Use specialized skills under `.claude/skills` when relevant.

* research-question

* experiment-design
* ml-experimentation

For significant research decisions, use the relevant skill before implementation.

## Communication

Challenge the developer when justified.

Do not ask unnecessary questions.

Use high scrutiny for high-impact decisions and low friction for routine work.
